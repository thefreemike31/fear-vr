F.E.A.R. VR v1.7.6 / guide reviewed October 9, 2026

Controller layouts

These layouts come from the same canonical mapping data used to compile the OpenXR bridge. They describe the default mapping profile, which may differ from the controller name printed on the hardware when a runtime emulates another profile.

Validation key: Meta / Oculus Touch is physically headset-validated. Every other mapping is specification-checked and compiled into the bridge but still needs feedback from matching hardware. System, Meta, Windows, and SteamVR dashboard buttons remain runtime-owned.
Pause with SteamVR: press the two lower face buttons together: Right A + Left X on Touch, or Right A + Left A on the Index mapping. Press them together rather than holding one first. The profile tables show the equivalent controls for other mappings. The normal mapped Menu button remains available when the runtime exposes it; a system/dashboard button may open the runtime instead.
Flying kick and slide (v1.7): On the jump/turn stick, flick up to jump and up again while airborne to kick. Ease the stick back between flicks; holding up does not repeat. For a slide, hold the movement stick forward and flick the jump/turn stick down twice within about half a second while grounded. Single up/down retain jump/crouch. Movement Stick/Southpaw chooses the sticks independently of weapon handedness. Kick/slide also work with Visible Body off. After a slide, tap crouch to stand if needed and space allows. A flying kick can open a closed door when the boot contacts it; normal locks and scripts still apply.
Fit Visible Body: Calibration & Body Fit > Fit Visible Body adjusts height, reach, shoulders, forward position and fullness. Touch A: previous field; B/Y: next; either stick left/right: adjust; down: reset field; X: exit. Adjustments save to your profile. Visible Body defaults On but preserves a saved Off. See CALIBRATION.md for the tutorial.
Mission and quick-save shortcuts (v1.6): Touch Y+B opens/closes the mission screen; release both before toggling again. Hold Y+A together for two seconds to quick save; hold X+B for two seconds to quick load the quick-save slot, not the latest checkpoint. Release either early to cancel. A progress bar shows the hold; a requested message is not proof that saving has completed. These physical combinations ignore handedness, Southpaw and action remapping. Save/load requires eligible single-player gameplay outside menus, dialogs, calibration and scripted camera control. Missing face buttons mean these shortcuts are unavailable; see each profile table for equivalents.
Remembered holsters (v1.7.6): Existing weapons stay in their slots across unrelated pickups and inventory changes. Compatible swaps inherit the outgoing weapon's place; layouts persist in saves. Heavy weapons and three-long-gun loadouts retain the normal back-slot rule. No calibration reset or button change is needed.
Trigger
Fire. Also selects menu items while pointing.
Grip / squeeze
Grab props, support weapons, climb, perform reload steps, or bring either free hand to your forehead with Grip released and squeeze once for the flashlight.
Standard sticks
Left moves. Right turns; vertical right-stick input jumps or crouches.
Southpaw option
Swaps locomotion and turn axes only. Weapon handedness selects a separate default/personal button layout.

Controls and settings

Controls & Layout: open Options > VR Settings > Controls & Layout to select your weapon hand and movement stick, toggle Tap to Walk, and choose whether a weapon-button tap cycles weapons or selects Unarmed. Button Layout / Remap shows the runtime-bound inputs and lets you move the six gameplay button roles. Used buttons exchange roles. Layouts are saved separately for each reported controller pair and weapon hand; Restore This Layout restores its defaults. The current controls and menu presentation are owner-accepted. Broader controller coverage awaits community feedback. These settings are included in this release.

Walk/Run and Medkit: start running; tap the mapped utility button to walk, then tap again to run. Hold it for a medkit. Tap to Walk defaults on and can be disabled on Controls & Layout. In Classic Sticky, holding the mapped weapon button opens the weapon selector, regardless of the tap option. In Grip & Holsters, both tap switching and the wheel are disabled. Triggers, Grips, menu navigation, and the pause chord retain their physical functions.

Physical actions: use Grip to grab, support, climb, and perform manual reload steps. In Grip & Holsters mode, reach to your hip, shoulder, or back weapon slot to draw or stow; the utility-hand chest socket holds throwables. Visible Holstered Weapons defaults to Yes; No hides passive body models and belt visuals without changing draw/reload interactions. Calibration references remain visible. Physical Pickups and Physical Weapon Swap are separate options under Immersion & Interaction. Either palm can touch eligible doors, buttons, and levers; the mapped Use button is also available.

Calibration & Body Fit: open Options > VR Settings > Calibration & Body Fit. From the main menu, calibration opens a dedicated room; during a campaign, it runs in your current level. Adjust height, holsters and pouches, Weapon Grip Calibration, or Advanced Sight Alignment. Body calibration includes a mirror. Grip and sight corrections have separate resets; sight alignment changes the aim ray without moving the weapon. This release includes the required room and material, controller-referenced hand fitting, and clearer calibration-menu text; the developer launcher and Level Select remain excluded.

VR Aiming: Dual Pistol Colors defaults to On (green/amber). Off uses the selected main colour for both pistols in every aim-indicator mode.

Headset Display: Render Resolution ranges from 50-200%, defaults to 125%, and applies on the next launch. Keep Centered (Recommended) projection and 100% View Width as the Quest-safe defaults. Headset Native and View Width adjustment remain experimental for other headsets. Restore Display Defaults resets all three values.

Guidance and comfort: Tutorial Cards and Interaction Cards have separate switches under Guidance Cards. Cards finish after a successful action or ten seconds of visible gameplay, without spending grenades, medkits or ammunition. Completed progress survives Continue, loading and settings. New Game or Replay Tutorial Cards resets progress. Reload cards require an actionable held weapon; weapon switching works during unfinished reloads. Supported pickups and belt magazines seat at visible hand contact. The received-damage indicator is independently optional under Comfort & Movement > Damage Indicator. Follow the contextual cards for weapon-specific reloads and physical interactions.
