# F.E.A.R. VR v1.7 - Full body and physical combat

- **Full-body IK:** articulated arms, torso, legs and procedural feet with body
  shadows. Enabled by default; an explicitly saved Off stays Off.
- **Body calibration suite:** Fit Visible Body adjusts height, reach, shoulder
  width, forward position and fullness in the mirror. Existing hand, holster,
  weapon-grip and sight tools remain available. [Body fitting guide](CALIBRATION.md#full-body-ik-and-fit-visible-body).
- **Flying kick:** flick the jump/turn stick up to jump, then up again while airborne.
- **Slide:** hold forward movement and flick the jump/turn stick down twice quickly
  while grounded. [Gesture details and controls](CONTROLS.md#flying-kick-and-slide).
- Weapon weight gives guns a more deliberate feel while retaining support-hand
  assistance. Improved body following, sleeve recovery and slow-motion transitions.
- Fixes room-scale movement becoming stuck after automatic recentering.
- Improved close door touch and physical strikes on padlocks and attached glass,
  including contact along a held gun's barrel/receiver.

Close the game, extract the complete ZIP and choose **Setup > Upgrade**. Keep
setup-files beside Setup; body meshes are required. Saves, calibration and language
backups are preserved. No calibration reset is needed. Package version: 1.7.0.

General language import remains included: original language ISO plus matching
1.08 language patch; all language editions are intended to work in theory, only
Spanish officially tested. The mirror's absent head remains a known limitation.
Auto Pickup/red-ladder follow-ups have offline coverage but no focused headset
acceptance. No separate Steam or new assembled-ZIP headset run is claimed.
See KNOWN-LIMITATIONS.md and the updated tutorials.
