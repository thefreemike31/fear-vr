# F.E.A.R. VR v1.7.1 - Important ladder hotfix

- **Fixes a progress-blocking ladder regression introduced with the body update.**
  Restores grabbing/climbing from water and correct standing exits onto platforms,
  preventing the reproduced under-platform landing and fall back into the pool.
- Keeps the visible body present during swimming and water-to-ladder transitions.
- Adds an optional experimental **OBS capture window** to the launcher: full
  right-eye capture, native-eye or fitted 16:9 framing, windowed/borderless modes
  and VR remaining live while OBS is in front.
  [Step-by-step OBS guide](INSTALLATION.md#recording-with-obs).

Close the game, extract the complete ZIP and choose **Setup > Upgrade**. Keep
setup-files beside Setup. Saves, calibration and language backups are preserved;
no reset is required. Package version: 1.7.1. Full-body IK, calibration, flying
kick/slide and general language import from earlier releases remain included.

The reproduced ladder failure was owner-headset accepted; every campaign ladder
and separate Steam/assembled-package headset coverage are not claimed. OBS capture
has known control/exit follow-ups and incomplete mode coverage; it is optional
and off by default. See [known limitations](KNOWN-LIMITATIONS.md).
Language import still accepts original language media plus its matching 1.08
patch in theory across languages; only Spanish has official real-media testing.
